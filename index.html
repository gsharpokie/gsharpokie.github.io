Perception and reality

How close is what we believe to what the data show?

Better or Worse?

Since 1980, has life in the U.S. gotten better or worse? Guess on 26 indicators, then see what the data show, and whether your misses leaned pessimistic.

What Do They Really Think?

Guess what Democrats and Republicans believe and who’s in each party, then compare your guesses with survey data and with what each side guessed about the other.

Play it →

Is Crime Up?

Most years, most Americans say crime is rising. Make three guesses, then compare 35 years of Gallup polls with what victimization surveys found.

Play it →

Guess the Share

Out of every 100 people in the U.S., how many are immigrants, and how has that changed since 1850? Make seven guesses, see each answer as 100 people, and compare with what most Americans guess.

Play it →

Who Emits the Carbon?

Which countries emit the most: in total, per person, or over history? And can an economy grow while its emissions shrink? Guess, then see the data.

Play it →

Who Has the Wealth?
